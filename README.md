# College Circular Central

1. Introduction

NotifyHub is a web-based College Notification Management System developed to make communication between college management and students easier, faster, and more effective.

In many colleges, important information such as announcements, event details, examination schedules, academic updates, and urgent notices is shared through notice boards, WhatsApp groups, or verbal communication. Sometimes students may miss important updates because information is shared across different platforms.

NotifyHub solves this problem by providing a single centralized platform where students can access all important college notifications in one place. It helps college administrators publish information and allows students to view updates whenever they need them.

2. Problem Statement

Traditional methods of sharing college information are not always effective. Students may miss important announcements because notices are displayed on physical notice boards or shared through different communication channels. Managing announcements and events manually can also be time-consuming for college administrators. There is a need for a centralized, user-friendly, and easily accessible platform that allows administrators to manage college notifications and helps students receive important updates without confusion or delay.
